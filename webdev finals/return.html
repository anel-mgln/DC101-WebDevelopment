RETURN POLICY

At Kloset, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy for any reason, please review our return policy below:

Eligibility for Returns
1. Items must be returned within 30 days of purchase.
2. Products should be unused, unwashed, and in original condition, including tags and packaging.
3. Final sale items, gift cards, and personalized products are non-returnable.

How to Initiate a Return
1. Contact us at returns@kloset.com with your order number and details about the item(s) you wish to return.
2. We will send you a prepaid shipping label.
3. Pack the item securely and include the original receipt or proof of purchase.

Refunds
1. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method within 7-10 business days after we receive your return.
2. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
For exchanges, please place a new order for the desired item and initiate a return for the original item.
